Order - 

order_id (UUID, PK): Unique identifier.

customer_id (UUID, FK): Link to the customer profile.

status (Enum): PENDING, PAID, SHIPPED, DELIVERED, CANCELLED, REFUNDED.

currency (String): ISO code (e.g., USD, EUR).

total_base_amount (Decimal): Price before taxes/shipping.

total_tax_amount (Decimal): Calculated tax.

total_shipping_amount (Decimal): Shipping cost.

total_discount_amount (Decimal): Total savings applied.

final_total_amount (Decimal): The actual amount charged.

payment_method_id (String): Reference to payment gateway token.

created_at / updated_at (Timestamp): Standard audit trail.


order_events - 

event_id (UUID, PK)

order_id (UUID, FK)

event_type (String): e.g., PAYMENT_SUCCESS, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY.

payload (JSONB): Raw metadata from the service that triggered the event.

occurred_at (Timestamp)

order_items - 

order_item_id (UUID, PK)

order_id (UUID, FK)

product_id (UUID, FK)

sku (String): Stock Keeping Unit at the time of purchase.

quantity (Integer)

unit_price (Decimal): Captured price (to protect against future price changes).

discount_amount (Decimal): Specific discount applied to this item.

tax_rate (Decimal): Percentage applied.
